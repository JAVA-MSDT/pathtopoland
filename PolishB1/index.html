Quick Start

New here? Do these 3 things first:

  1. 📊 Assess your level: Take the free placement test at polskina5.pl to know where you stand right now.
  2. 📥 Download a past exam: Get a real B1 past exam from certyfikatpolski.pl and read through it without answering anything — circle every unknown word and structure.
  3. 📅 Follow the 12-week plan: Go to the Study Plan and start with Week 1 today. Each block takes just 15–20 minutes a day.
