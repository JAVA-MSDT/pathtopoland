Grammar Tools — Narzędzia Gramatyczne

Two AI-powered reference tools in one place. Look up any Polish verb to get its full conjugation table with its aspect pair, or look up any noun to see its gender and complete 7-case declension.

AI-Powered Verb Conjugator Noun Declension Free
🤖 Instant AI grammar reference — free, no sign-in required: Type any Polish verb or noun and click the button. Results appear in a few seconds — full tables, aspect pairs, and adjective agreement tips included.

🔵 Verb Conjugator — Koniugacja Czasownika

Enter any Polish verb in its infinitive form (the base form ending in -ć or -c) — for example mówić, iść, kupić, mieszkać, wiedzieć. You will get the full conjugation table across all tenses, plus the aspect pair if one exists.

🔵 Conjugate a verb — Skoniuguj czasownik

Type the infinitive form of the verb you want to look up. The AI will return all tenses and the aspect pair automatically.

🔤 Polish spell-check — how to enable it in your browser

As you type, Polish spell-check automatically marks real typing mistakes — for example mówć instead of mówić. Correctly spelled Polish words are left untouched.

🟢 Noun & Adjective Lookup — Odmiana

Enter any Polish noun — for example kot, kobieta, miasto, brat, dziecko — to get its gender and full 7-case declension. Or enter any adjective — for example dobry, nowy, odważny, piękny — to get its full declension across all genders and cases.

🟢 Look up a noun or adjective — Sprawdź odmianę

Type any Polish noun or adjective in its base (nominative) form. For nouns: full 7-case table + adjective agreement tip. For adjectives: all genders and cases in one table.
