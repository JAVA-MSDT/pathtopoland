🔤Polish spell-check — how to enable it in your browser

As you type, Polish spell-check automatically marks real typing mistakes with a red underline — for example jestm instead of jestem. Correctly spelled Polish words like kawę or dobrego are left untouched.

⚠️ Seeing red underlines under correct Polish words like jestem or kawę?

Your browser doesn't have the Polish dictionary installed yet. The fix takes 30 seconds and only needs to be done once:

Chrome / Edge — one-time setup:
  1. Open browser Settings → Languages
  2. Click "Add languages" → search Polish → Add
  3. Toggle on "Use this language for spell check"
  4. Reload this page — done ✅

💡 Once Polish is added, the spell-checker works correctly everywhere in your browser — not just on this page.
