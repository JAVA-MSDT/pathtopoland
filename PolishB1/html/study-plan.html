12-Week Preparation Plan

A fully actionable, day-by-day study guide built around the official B1 exam structure. Each week block builds on the previous one — follow in order for best results.

12 Weeks 15–20 min/day Structured Free Resources

One-Time Setup — Do This Before Week 1

30-minute setup that makes the whole plan work

  • Create a free Anki account at ankiweb.net — your flashcard platform for all 12 weeks. Use it every day, even just 5 minutes.
  • Bookmark the past exam archive: certyfikatpolski.pl/o-egzaminie/testy-egzaminacyjne-z-poprzednich-lat/ — you will need it in Weeks 8, 10, and 12.
  • Get a dedicated paper notebook for written exercises — writing Polish case endings by hand accelerates memory retention significantly more than typing.
  • Install Duolingo (free) as a 5-minute daily warm-up supplement only, not as your primary study method.
These four steps take 30 minutes and are the difference between a plan that works and one that stalls. Do them before Day 1.

Days 8–14 (Week 2): Time Expressions + "z" Collocations

Learn and say aloud these time expressions each morning — they use Instrumental with no preposition:

  • rano — in the morning | wieczorem — in the evening | nocą — at night
  • latem — in summer | zimą — in winter | wiosną — in spring | jesienią — in autumn
  • wieczorami — in the evenings (habitual) | rankiem — in the morning (formal) | nocami — at nights

Learn these "z + Instrumental" collocations — they appear in both reading and writing tasks:

  • kawa z mlekiem — coffee with milk | herbata z cytryną — tea with lemon | chleb z masłem — bread with butter
  • pojechać z rodziną — to go with family | mieszkać z przyjacielem — to live with a friend
  • rozmawiać z szefem — to talk with the boss | wyjść z psem — to go out with the dog
  • z przyjemnością — with pleasure | z całego serca — with all my heart | z góry dziękuję — thank you in advance

📅 Weeks 8–9 — Reading Strategy + Writing Practice

Goal: Complete two timed Reading subtests from past exam papers. Produce four written texts at B1 standard using the templates from the Writing guide.

The 3-Pass Reading Strategy (use on every reading task)

  1. Pass 1 — Questions first (2 min): Read every question and answer option before the text. Underline the key content word in each. Your brain will search for those patterns automatically while reading.
  2. Pass 2 — Skim for structure (3 min): Read only the first and last sentence of each paragraph. This gives you the skeleton and tells you which paragraph is relevant to which question.
  3. Pass 3 — Targeted reading (remaining time): Read only the paragraphs relevant to your remaining unanswered questions. Do not re-read the entire text.
Task TypeWhat it testsKey strategy
True / False / Not StatedIs the claim confirmed, denied, or not mentioned?"Not stated" = the text neither confirms nor denies it. If you hesitate between False and Not Stated — if the text never addresses it at all, choose Not Stated.
Heading matchingMatch one heading to each paragraphFind the ONE main topic per paragraph — ignore supporting detail. Match by topic, not by repeated surface words (those are traps).
Multiple choice A/B/CChoose the answer supported by the textEliminate first. The correct answer must be explicitly supported in the text — do not use general knowledge.
Sentence gap fillChoose which sentence fits in the gapRead one sentence before AND one after the gap. Check connector words at the start of each option (jednak, dlatego, ponadto).

📅 Week 10 — Słuchanie (Listening Subtest)

Goal: Complete 3 full past-exam Listening subtests. Build the habit of identifying main ideas and specific details from natural-speed Polish speech within the 30-minute window.

The 3 Recording Types on the Listening Subtest

Recording typeWhat you hearWhat you answer
Short monologues (5–7)Announcements, voicemails, weather reports, radio ads — each 20–40 secondsMatch each to its topic / context from a list of options
Two-person dialogue (1–2)Everyday conversations: at the doctor's, booking a hotel, discussing plansTrue / False / Not Stated, or multiple choice about main points
Longer monologue / interview (1)Radio interview, presentation, or news segment — 2–3 minutesFill in missing information in sentences, or multiple choice about specific details

The 3-Step Listening Method

  1. Pre-listen — questions first: Read all questions and underline the key content word in each option. This primes your brain for what to catch.
  2. First listen — main ideas only: Focus on WHO is speaking, WHAT is happening, and WHY. The recording plays twice — save detail-hunting for the second play.
  3. Second listen — specific details: Now you know the context. Target the underlined keywords. Write your answer the moment you hear the information.

📅 Week 11 — Mówienie (Speaking Subtest)

Goal: Record yourself completing all three speaking task types. Achieve a minimum 90-second unbroken monologue on at least 2 different topics without restarts or long pauses.

What Happens in the Exam Room

The oral exam is conducted with one examiner and typically one other candidate. Three tasks, approximately 15 minutes total:

  1. Task 1 — Photo Description (4–5 min): You describe a photograph, speculate about the people/situation, and answer 2 follow-up questions from the examiner.
  2. Task 2 — Role-play (4–5 min): You receive a card with a realistic situation (e.g., you are a customer with a complaint). You role-play with the examiner.
  3. Task 3 — Opinion / Discussion (4–5 min): The examiner gives you a statement on a social topic. You give your opinion with reasons, and respond to counter-arguments.

Task 1 Practice — Photo Description Structure

Find 5 photos of everyday Polish life. Record yourself for 60–90 seconds per photo using this fixed structure:

  1. "Na zdjęciu widzę [people / place / objects]."
  2. "Na pierwszym planie jest/są... Osoba po lewej stronie..."
  3. "W tle widać... Wydaje mi się, że to jest..."
  4. "Myślę, że ta osoba... / Wydaje mi się, że oni rozmawiają o..."
  5. "To zdjęcie kojarzy mi się z... / Podobna sytuacja zdarzyła mi się, kiedy..."

Task 3 Practice — 8 Most Common B1 Opinion Topics

Structure: Opinion (1 sentence) → Reason 1 (2 sentences) → Reason 2 (2 sentences) → Conclusion (1 sentence). Record yourself, listen back.

Days 80–84: The Final Week Before Exam

  • Day 80: Anki — review all cards accumulated over 12 weeks. Sort by hardest (lowest success rate) and spend 45 min on those 30 cards only. Do not learn new vocabulary this week.
  • Day 81: Re-read the Writing Templates. Write one short practice text without a timer to warm up the muscle memory.
  • Day 82: 20 minutes of Polskie Radio — passive exposure to the language rhythm only.
  • Day 83: One photo description + one opinion monologue aloud. Stop there. No studying in the evening. Rest.
  • Day 84 (Exam Eve): Prepare all documents. Check the address and transport route to your exam center. Set your alarm. Sleep at a normal time — no studying.
