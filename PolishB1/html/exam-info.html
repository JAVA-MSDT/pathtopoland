How & Where to Take the Polish B1 Exam

Everything you need to register, choose the right exam type, and walk into the exam room prepared — with verified Warsaw center details and registration tips.

2 Exam Types Warsaw Centers State: ~740 PLN TELC: ~1150 PLN

Two Routes to a Recognized B1 Certificate

You have two primary paths to secure a certificate accepted by the Polish Office for Foreigners for your Long-Term EU Resident Permit. The State Exam is cheaper and reusable for future citizenship applications, but fills up in minutes. The TELC exam is more expensive but runs nearly every month and processes results faster. Both are fully valid for the residence permit. Read the full comparison below before you book.

Option A — State Exam (Państwowy Egzamin Certyfikatowy)

How it works

  • How it works: Held on strict dates 3–4 times per year across registered academic locations. Registration windows fill up nationwide in under 10 minutes. The exam is administered in 5 separate subtests: Listening, Reading, Grammar, Writing, and Speaking.
  • Cost: Approx. 650 PLN exam fee + a mandatory 90 PLN certificate issuing fee (total ~740 PLN).
  • Results: Takes 2–3 months to issue after the exam date.
  • Passing threshold: You must score at least 51% in each of the 5 subtests separately — a strong score in one subtest cannot compensate for a weak one.
KEY ADVANTAGE: Only the State certificate (Certyfikat Języka Polskiego) can be reused later for Polish Citizenship applications. If long-term citizenship is your goal, this is the route to choose.

State Exam Registration Tips:
  • Session dates are announced approximately 3 months in advance at certyfikatpolski.pl — bookmark the site and check it in February, June, and September.
  • Registration opens on a published date and can close within minutes — prepare your personal data and payment details in advance so you can complete the form instantly when registration opens.
  • Typical Warsaw session windows: Spring (May/June) and Autumn (November/December).
  • If Warsaw fills up, the nearest fallback cities with regular sessions are Kraków, Wrocław, and Poznań.
  • You choose your testing center during registration — centers in smaller cities often have more available spots.

Option B — TELC B1-B2 Dual Exam

How it works

  • How it works: Managed by licensed private vocational schools. Conducted frequently — nearly every month in major cities like Warsaw, Kraków, and Wrocław. The TELC exam is a dual-level format: the same test, graded to two thresholds.
  • Cost: Typically 1,000–1,200 PLN (physical certificate is fully included).
  • Results: Digital PDF certificate within 6–8 weeks.
  • Passing threshold: 50% of total points overall — not per subtest. A strong performance in one section can compensate for a weaker one.
  • Dual-level bonus: If your overall score reaches 60% or above, you are awarded a B2 certificate — same exam, higher reward for higher performance.
IMPORTANT: The TELC certificate is accepted for the Long-Term EU Resident Permit, but is NOT accepted for Polish Citizenship applications later. If citizenship is a future goal, choose Option A.

TELC-Specific Notes:
  • The TELC written exam runs for 115 minutes: Listening (25 min), Reading (40 min), Language Elements / Grammar (20 min), Writing (30 min). After a break there is the oral exam: 20 min preparation + 16 min conducted in pairs.
  • The TELC passing threshold is 50% of total points overall — not per subtest like the state exam. This means a strong performance in one section can compensate for a weaker one.
  • Scoring above 60% overall earns a B2 certificate instead of B1 — same exam, higher reward for higher performance.
